Home Calendar

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Home Calendar is a small private app that shows one household its own calendar and chores. This page explains what Google data it uses, where that data goes and how to remove it.

What data it reads

  • Google Tasks, read-only. The app asks for one Google permission only: the Google Tasks read-only scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks.readonly. It reads the task lists and tasks of the Google accounts that signed in, to show them as chores. It cannot create, change or delete tasks.
  • Google Calendar, by private iCal address. The app reads the household's own private Google Calendar iCal addresses, which the household enters itself. These are read-only feeds. The app asks for no Google Calendar permission and cannot change any calendar.

The app does not read email, contacts, files, location or any other Google data.

Where the data goes

The data is fetched straight from Google to the household's own device. It is cached only in the app's private storage on that device, so it can be shown between refreshes.

Nothing is sent to anyone else. There is no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and the data is never sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

The only hosts the app contacts are:

  • calendar.google.com (the private iCal feeds)
  • tasks.googleapis.com (Google Tasks, read-only)
  • oauth2.googleapis.com (refreshing the sign-in token)

Signing in happens once, on Google's own sign-in page. The resulting token stays with the household and is not sent anywhere else.

How long it is kept

Cached data is kept only until it is replaced by the next refresh or wiped. Sign-in tokens are kept only until access is revoked or the tokens are wiped.

How to remove access and data

  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time under Google Account › Security › Third-party access (sometimes shown as "Your connections to third-party apps & services").
  • The app's set-up tool has a wipe command that removes the stored tokens and calendar addresses.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data also removes everything it stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Home Calendar's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show the household its own calendar and chores. It is not used for advertising, not transferred to others, not used to train any AI or machine-learning model, and not read by any person other than the household itself.

Children

The app is used only by the household that runs it and collects nothing from anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
