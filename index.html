Home Calendar

A small private app that shows one household its own calendar and chores.

What it is

Home Calendar is a small personal app. It shows the household's own calendar and chores on a device in the household's home.

Calendar and chores

The calendar reads the household's own private Google Calendar iCal addresses. The chores read the household's own Google Tasks lists, read-only. Nothing is ever written back to Google.

The data goes straight from Google to the household's own device. There is no server in between, no account with us, no analytics and no ads. The privacy policy has the details.

Who it is for

Only the household that runs it. It is not a product, it is not sold, and it is not offered to the public. The Google sign-in exists only so the household's own device can read the household's own task lists.
